# Contributing

For a material or hard-to-reverse engineering choice, consider opening a
**Decision proposal** issue before implementation. An issue is useful when the
choice needs research or discussion, but it is not required for a decision to
be valid.

An accepted decision record must:

- use `decisions/0000-template.md`;
- have a unique, permanent decision ID;
- stand alone with sufficient context, rationale, and consequences;
- link its discussion issue when one exists;
- name any decisions it supersedes;
- update `decisions/README.md`; and
- update both sides of any supersession relationship.

Decision records are historical artifacts. Amend minor errors in place, but
use a new decision to change engineering direction.
